# Colour Knobs

A colour toy for toddlers. Turn three animal knobs for red, green and blue to mix the background colour.

Play: https://signalrecordsuk.github.io/colour-knobs/

- Tap a knob to brighten it in steps, or drag round it like a real knob (or two-finger swipe) to turn it.
- The paw button swaps between seven groups of three animals: jungle, safari, sea, farm, pets, forest and dinosaurs.
- The paw button also shows a colour to aim for: turn the knobs until the page matches it for confetti and a fanfare. Each press picks a new colour.
- Difficulty: hold the paw and press a knob (top easy, middle medium, bottom hard), or shift-click the paw on a computer to cycle.
  - Easy: each knob has 6 stops and snaps to the nearest when let go.
  - Medium: 11 stops, also snapping.
  - Hard: no snapping; get within 2 on each of red, green and blue.
